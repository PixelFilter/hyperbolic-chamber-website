Origins

A project rooted in sonic exploration

Hyperbolic Chamber began as a personal outlet for pure creative energy, then opened outward into a shared platform for DJs willing to experiment, take risks, and chase texture wherever it leads.

It started with a simple, selfish idea: to create a platform where I could play music just to play music, a space for pure creative energy to flow, unrestrained.

It was a place of refuge where the mountains of music I had collected over the years could be experimented with. The Chamber became my podium to perform, to explore, to shape, and to share them with anyone willing to listen.

But it didn't stop there. The idea evolved organically. I wanted to open this space up, to feature other DJs, and to offer them the same freedom I had while expanding my own musical horizons.

It was clear I needed PixelFilter by my side. A DJ, yes, but more importantly, a friend whose musical journey had always run parallel to my own.

Our odyssey goes back to one fateful night: a rave, a spark of something unspoken, a telepathic connection, and from that moment on, our path was set.
